## About

Undreamt is an early-stage, community-driven initiative focused on building practical software and sharing technical knowledge in the open. Rather than centering on a single product, our work spans developer tooling, cloud-native infrastructure, AI systems, and educational resources.

We operate with a transparent, iterative model—documenting our architectural decisions, publishing experimental prototypes alongside core projects, and refining our priorities as the community grows.

## Focus Areas

- **Developer Tooling & Frameworks:** Modular libraries, runtimes, and utilities designed to improve developer workflows.
- **Cloud-Native Systems & Infrastructure:** Scalable architectures, containerization, and distributed system primitives.
- **AI & Agent Systems:** Secure execution environments, policy guardrails, and applied machine learning tooling.
- **Technical Education:** Open learning resources, architectural breakdowns, and hands-on guides.
- **Knowledge & Information Systems:** Structured tools for organizing, retrieving, and verifying information.
- **Applied Research:** Exploratory prototypes and open-source experimentation.

## Engineering Principles

- **Learn in Public:** Share progress, design trade-offs, and post-mortems openly.
- **Thoughtful Design:** Prioritize clean architecture, secure defaults, and long-term maintainability.
- **Pragmatism Over Complexity:** Choose straightforward solutions that solve real problems without unnecessary abstraction.
- **Transparent Maturity:** Clearly communicate whether a project is experimental, under active development, or production-ready.
- **Accessible Collaboration:** Lower the barrier to entry through clear documentation and structured contribution workflows.

## Contributing & Community

Undreamt welcomes developers, researchers, designers, writers, and learners of all experience levels. Meaningful contributions extend beyond writing code—reporting bugs, improving documentation, testing releases, and participating in architectural discussions all help move projects forward.

Each repository maintains its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, license, and stability status. Please review the relevant repository guidelines before submitting pull requests or opening issues.

## Current Status

Undreamt is in its foundational phase. Active repositories range from early-stage experiments to structured frameworks under continuous development. Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/jMPraFRht) or explore our pinned repositories to follow ongoing work and get involved.
